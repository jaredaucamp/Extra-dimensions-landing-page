Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Extra Dimensions (Pty) Ltd ("Extra Dimensions", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information we collect

When you submit a form or message us on WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your business name
  • The number of SIM cards or staff members you tell us about

We do not collect payment card details through this website. Payments are handled via EFT, invoice, or a third-party payment processor that manages its own card security.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your enquiry or order
  • To set up and manage your data bundle and SIM activation
  • To send billing invoices and service updates
  • To improve our service and respond to support requests

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share necessary details with MTN solely to activate and manage your SIM cards on our reseller account.

How we store your information

Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized Extra Dimensions staff. We retain your information for as long as you remain a customer, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for billing and legal record-keeping.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. These tools may collect non-identifying information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at info@extra-dimensions.co.za or WhatsApp +27 60 133 8237.
