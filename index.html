FAQ

Questions before you sign up

Straight answers to what business owners ask most.

All SIM cards on your plan are registered to your business account and linked to the same 50GB pool. When an employee uses data on their phone, it deducts from the shared balance. No individual caps per SIM by default. The whole 50GB is available to whoever needs it most.
No hard limit on SIMs. Most of our clients run 3 to 15 people comfortably on 50GB per month. If your team is larger or your usage is heavier (video calls, large file transfers), we can move you to a higher bundle. Talk to us and we will match you to the right option.
The 50GB bundle is prepaid and renewed monthly. You pay, your team uses it, you renew. No 24-month contracts locking you in. You stay because the value is there, not because you signed something.
Data stops automatically. No surprise charges, no uncapped billing. You can buy a top-up bundle at any time to keep your team going, or wait for your next monthly renewal. We can also set usage alerts so you know before it hits zero.
Fill in the form below or message us on WhatsApp. We contact you within a few hours to confirm details, handle RICA, and take payment. Most businesses are fully active within 24 to 48 hours. No branch visits, no lengthy applications.
Staff already on MTN can have their existing SIMs linked to your business pool without changing their number. Employees on other networks can use a dedicated MTN data SIM for the shared bundle. We walk you through the best setup for your team's situation.
