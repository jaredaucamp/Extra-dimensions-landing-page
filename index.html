What is a 50GB shared data bundle?

Instead of buying individual data for each employee, your business gets one 50GB pool. Every staff member's SIM card draws from the same reserve, so nobody on your team runs dry while others have unused gigabytes sitting idle.

You see everything in one place: total usage, who used what, and how much is left. No more juggling ten different accounts or chasing airtime receipts.

FAQ

Common questions

Answers to what business owners ask before they sign up.

Each staff member gets a SIM card that is registered to your business account. All those SIMs are linked to a single 50GB pool. When an employee uses data on their phone, it deducts from the shared balance. There are no individual caps per SIM by default. The 50GB is available to whoever needs it most.
There is no hard limit on the number of SIMs. Most of our business clients run between 3 and 15 users comfortably on 50GB per month. If your team is larger or your usage is heavier, we can advise on a higher-capacity bundle. Contact us and we will match you to the right option.
The 50GB data bundle is prepaid and renewed monthly. You pay for the bundle, your team uses it, and you renew when you need to. There is no long-term contract locking you in. You stay because it works, not because you signed a 24-month agreement.
Data usage stops automatically. No surprise bills, no uncapped charges. You can purchase a top-up bundle at any time to keep your team connected, or wait until your next monthly renewal. We can also help you set usage alerts so you know when the pool is running low before it hits zero.
Fill in the short form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. We will contact you within a few hours to confirm your details, handle the RICA process, and arrange payment. Most businesses are fully activated within 24 to 48 hours. No lengthy applications, no branch visits required.
The data bundle works with MTN SIM cards linked to your business account. Employees who already have MTN numbers can have their SIMs registered to the pool without changing their number. For staff on other networks, we can issue MTN SIMs for data use. We walk you through the best setup for your specific situation.
